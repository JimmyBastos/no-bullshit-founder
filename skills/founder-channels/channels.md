# Channels

The channel list follows *Traction* by Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares
(2015), grouped and trimmed for an early test. The notes say when each one
tends to fit. They are starting points; the evidence in step 1 decides.

## Fast enough to test in about two weeks

| channel | fits when | watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search ads (Google, Bing) | people already search for a solution | costly keywords; needs a clear landing page |
| Social ads (Meta: Facebook and Instagram; TikTok) | B2C, visual or emotional products, buyers who don't search yet | needs many different creatives; optimize for sales or leads, not clicks |
| LinkedIn ads | B2B, buyers defined by job title or company | high cost per click; small budgets get little data |
| Direct outreach by the founder | B2B, few buyers, high price | messages are sent by a person, not a bot; LinkedIn bans automation |
| In person (visits, fairs, events) | local businesses, B2B, trust-based markets | slow to scale, but fast to learn |
| Communities (groups, forums, associations) | buyers gather around the problem | each group's rules on promotion; give before you ask |
| Founder's network | finding first users and interviews | these are warm sales: they show the product works, not that a market exists |
| WhatsApp | markets where buyers already talk to businesses there (Brazil) | needs opt-in and approved templates for marketing messages |
| Email to an opted-in list | the user already has a list, or collects one on the page | consent required; set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC first |
| Partners and resellers | someone already sells to these buyers | they want a margin; slower to set up |
| Marketplaces (Mercado Livre, Amazon, app stores) | buyers already shop there for this | fees, and the marketplace owns the customer |

## Slow: better for growth (step 6)

| channel | fits when | watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO and blog | steady search demand, content can answer the questions buyers ask | months before results |
| Organic social (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, YouTube) | the founder can post often and the topic is visual or opinion-led | slow to build an audience; useful to test hooks, for example with Instagram trial reels |
| PR and press | there is a real story | unpredictable |
| Referral programs | there are happy customers already | needs customers first |
| Engineering as marketing (free tools, calculators) | buyers search for a tool around the problem | build time |
| Affiliates and influencers | the audience matches the buyer exactly | fake followers; agree on pay per sale |

## Questions that sort it out

1. Do buyers search for a solution? Yes: search ads first. No: go where they
   already spend time.
2. Is the price high and the number of buyers small? Then talk to them
   directly before spending on ads.
3. Can someone show the product in a few seconds of video or one picture?
   Then social ads and visual platforms are candidates.
4. Is there someone who already sells to these buyers? Then a partner may be
   the shortest path.
