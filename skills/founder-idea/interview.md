# Interview guide

Based on *The Mom Test* by Rob Fitzpatrick (2013). The idea: people lie to be
nice when you ask about your idea, but they rarely lie about what they did
last week. So ask about their life, not your idea.

## Three rules

1. Talk about their life, not your idea. Don't pitch until the end, if at all.
2. Ask about specific past events, not opinions or the future. "When did this
   last happen?" beats "Would you use...?"
3. Listen more than you talk. Silence is fine; let them fill it.

## Before the call

- Write down the one or two assumptions this interview should test. They come
  from the top of the list in `founder/idea.md`.
- Know what would change your mind.

## The script (about 20 minutes)

Adapt the words. Keep the order.

1. **Context.** "Tell me about how you handle <the area> today."
2. **Last time.** "When was the last time <the problem> came up? Walk me
   through what happened."
3. **Cost.** "What did that cost you, in money, time or hassle?"
4. **Workaround.** "What do you do about it now? What have you tried?" If they
   pay for something, ask what and how much.
5. **Search.** "Did you look for a solution? Where did you look? What did you
   find?" This feeds the channel step.
6. **Who decides.** "If you were to fix this, who else would be involved? Who
   pays?" For B2B, ask about the budget and how buying works.
7. **Priority.** "Where does this rank among the things you deal with?"
8. **Close.** "Who else should I talk to about this?" An introduction is level
   3 evidence.

Only at the end, if it fits: describe the solution in one sentence and ask
for a commitment, not an opinion. For example, a follow-up call, a trial, or
a pre-order. Their answer is the evidence.

## Signals

| strong (counts) | weak (does not count) |
| --- | --- |
| tells a specific recent story without prompting | "that's a great idea" |
| already pays for a workaround, or spends hours on it | "I would definitely use that" |
| has looked for solutions | "I might pay around..." |
| introduces you to others | agreeing with everything |
| commits time or money now | "let me know when it's ready" |
